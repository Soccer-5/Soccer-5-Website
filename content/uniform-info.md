**Away Team = Light Jersey**

**Home Team = Dark Jersey**

The HOME team will change jerseys or wear pinnies if the referee determines the
jersey colors are too similar. There are some clubs with only one jersey, so if
playing these teams, the opponent could choose the color of the jersey they will
wear in advance of heading to the game to avoid issues.
