The complete Rules of Competition for Soccer-5 are [available here]({{ rules_url }}).

You can find a [summary of the rules here]({{ rules_summary_url }}).

It is the responsibility of each coach, referee, and player to know our league
Rules of Competition.

In accordance with USSF, the Soccer-5 Rules of Competition are based on the
current IFAB rules, known as [The Laws of the Game](https://theifab.com).  A
friendly, easy to read version maintained by IFAB can be found at [footballrules.com](https://footballrules.com).

The league expects coaches and spectators to treat our referees with respect.  Coaches can
report concerns about referees to their club referee assignor, or the league via the
referee feedback form.  Parents who have questions or concerns should raise them with their
player's coach and not approach referees at any time.
