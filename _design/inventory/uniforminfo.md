Soccer 5 Uniforms

Away Team = Light Jersey

Home Team = Dark Jersey

The HOME team will change jerseys or wear pinnies if the referee determines the jersey colors are too similar. There are some clubs with only one jersey, so if playing these teams, the opponent could choose the color of the jersey they will wear in advance of heading to the game to avoid issues.

Canby = two jerseys, one navy blue and one white

Clackamas = two jerseys, one red jersey and one black jersey

Colton = two jerseys, one green/white and one gold
